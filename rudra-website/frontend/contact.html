Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple website might take 2-4 weeks, while larger projects could take 8-12 weeks or more. We'll provide a detailed timeline after the discovery phase.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer flexible pricing options including fixed-price projects, time-and-materials, and retainer agreements. Each project is unique, so we provide custom quotes based on your specific requirements.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive post-launch support including maintenance, updates, optimization, and 24/7 technical assistance. We're committed to your long-term success.

Can you work with existing systems?

Yes, we can integrate with and enhance existing systems, migrate from legacy platforms, or work with specific technologies you're already using. We're experienced with diverse tech stacks.

How do you ensure project communication?

We maintain regular communication through scheduled meetings, detailed documentation, and a dedicated project dashboard. You'll always have visibility into project progress and milestones.

What happens if I'm not satisfied?

Client satisfaction is our priority. If you're not satisfied with the results, we'll work with you to make revisions and adjustments until you're completely happy with the deliverables.
